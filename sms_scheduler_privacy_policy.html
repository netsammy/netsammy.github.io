Privacy Policy

Official Privacy Policy for SMS Scheduler & Email Scheduler Applications

Effective Date: August 08, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how our SMS Scheduler Application and Email Scheduler Application ("we", "us", or "our") handle user data, security, and personal privacy. We are committed to absolute data privacy and transparency.

2. On-Device Storage & Permissions

Our applications operate primarily on-device. Data is stored locally in your device's encrypted application sandbox. We request only the minimum required Android permissions:

SEND_SMS

Required solely to transmit SMS messages that you explicitly compose and schedule to your designated recipients at the designated delivery time.

READ_CONTACTS

Accessed strictly locally on your device to display phonebook contacts when using the contact picker feature. Contacts are never collected, saved externally, or transmitted off your device.

INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Utilized by Email Scheduler to transmit scheduled emails via SMTP/REST API, and by SMS Scheduler if optional Twilio Cloud Gateway mode is enabled.

SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Utilized to guarantee precise millisecond delivery timing for scheduled messages and display local confirmation status alerts.

3. Data Protection & Privacy Rights

  • 100% Local Data Control: All scheduled messages, templates, history logs, and group lists remain stored locally on your device.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: We do not track user behavior, collect diagnostic telemetry, or sell personal information.
  • Full Data Erasure: You can clear history logs, delete templates, or purge all app data at any time via Settings or Android App Info.

4. Cloud Services (Optional)

If you voluntarily enable optional cloud features (such as Twilio Cloud Gateway Mode or custom SMTP Email Servers), your composed payload (recipient and body text) is transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS directly to the specified service providers. Please consult Twilio's Privacy Policy for details on Twilio API data processing.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
