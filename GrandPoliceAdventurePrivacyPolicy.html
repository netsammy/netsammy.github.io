Privacy Policy

Grand Police Adventure · Netsam · Last updated 16 August 2026
The short version. Grand Police Adventure is a single-player driving game. There are no accounts, no logins, no chat, and no servers of ours. We do not collect, store or transmit any personal information about you. The game does not know who you are.

What the game stores

Game progress — your best score and which missions you have unlocked — is saved to local storage on your own device. It never leaves the device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number
  • We do not require an account or a login
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties
  • We run no analytics or crash-reporting service

Permissions

Windows. The game requests no special permissions. It makes one outbound request, to fetch the promotional listing described under “Promotions for our other games” below.

Android. The game requests INTERNET, used only to load advertisements. No gameplay feature needs a network connection — the game is fully playable offline.

Advertising (Android version only)

The Android version uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google, not by us. To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier
  • Approximate, non-precise location derived from IP address
  • Device model, operating system version and language
  • Which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them

This data is collected directly by Google under Google's own privacy policy. We do not receive it and cannot access it.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from partner sites and apps

Your choices

You can limit ad personalisation at any time without affecting gameplay: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID, or Opt out of Ads Personalisation.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent prompt before any personalised advertising, as required by the GDPR and Google's EU User Consent Policy. Declining does not restrict any part of the game.

The Windows version contains no third-party advertising and no AdMob.

Promotions for our other games

All versions of the game may show a “More Games” panel listing other apps and games published by us. This is self-promotion, not a third-party ad network.

To keep that list current without shipping an app update, the game downloads a small public file from netsammy.github.io. This is a plain one-way download of a text file. It sends us no information about you, sets no cookie, and reads no advertising identifier. As with any web request, the server hosting the file (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address as a normal part of delivering it — we do not receive or store it.

If you tap one of those promotions, your device opens the relevant store page in your browser or store app. What happens there is governed by that store's own privacy policy.

If a promotion is ever a paid or affiliate placement rather than one of our own apps, it will be clearly labelled as such on the card itself.

Children

Grand Police Adventure is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this game, contact us and we will act on it.

In-app purchases

The game contains no in-app purchases. If that changes, purchases will be handled entirely by the Google Play Store or the Microsoft Store. Payment details are processed by those stores and are never seen by us.

Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Local game progress is removed when you uninstall the game.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or erase — but you are welcome to contact us with any question.
