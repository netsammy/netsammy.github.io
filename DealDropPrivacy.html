DealDrop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

DealDrop ("the app") is a price-tracking application published by Abdus Global. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is used.

Data we collect

We collect the following data when you use the app:

  • An anonymous account identifier, created automatically the first time you open the app. It is not linked to your name, email address, or phone number.
  • The products you choose to track and any target price you set for them.
  • Taps on outbound store links, which we record so we can confirm our affiliate links are working.

You do not create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, or any payment card details. The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not require you to sign in.

Affiliate disclosure

When you open a product link from DealDrop to AliExpress, we use an affiliate link. If you make a purchase, AliExpress may pay us a commission. This never changes the price you pay.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show relevant ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings. See Google's advertising policy for details.

Data sharing

We share data only with the service providers required to run the app: Supabase (database and anonymous authentication) and Google AdMob (advertising). We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your anonymous account is active. Because the app has no sign-in, uninstalling it ends our ability to associate any further activity with you. To request deletion of the data already associated with your anonymous identifier, email us at the address below and we will complete the deletion within 30 days.

Children

DealDrop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Features that would collect additional data — such as price-drop push notifications or an optional paid subscription — are not part of the current version, and this policy will be updated before any such feature is released.
