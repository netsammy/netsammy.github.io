Privacy Policy for Anagram Arena

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

Welcome to Anagram Arena ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while you enjoy our 100-player Battle Royale word puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you play our game on Android and other platforms.

1. Information We Collect

  • Device & Authentication Data: Anonymous device IDs generated locally to persist game scores, leaderboard rankings, and player statistics without requiring personal identity.
  • Gameplay & Match Analytics: Real-time match scores, word submissions, round progression, and game performance statistics.
  • Technical Log Data: Connection IP address, ping latency, and app version for server matchmaking optimization and anti-cheat enforcement.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To match you into live 100-player game lobbies and compute real-time leaderboards.
  • To detect and prevent dictionary automation, cheating, and malicious activity.
  • To save your career statistics, unlocked cosmetics, and player progression.

3. Third-Party Services

Our game utilizes trusted third-party service providers:

  • Google Play Services: Authentication, Google Play Games services, and distribution.
  • Nakama Server (Heroic Labs): Open-source multiplayer backend engine for match coordination.

4. Children's Privacy

Anagram Arena is a general audience word puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Player scores and anonymous device session tokens are stored securely. You may request deletion of your player data at any time by contacting our support team.
